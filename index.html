About

I hold a degree in Computer Engineering and a Master of Science in Computer Science. I currently teach Design and Programming Methodologies at Universidad de Chile.

My work focuses on metaheuristic optimization and software engineering, primarily using Kotlin and Scala. I also have experience with Java, Python, and C#.
